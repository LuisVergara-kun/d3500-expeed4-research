# Features

What the research build surfaces on the Nikon D3500, **ordered by what matters most to a
photographer**. Every capability below is **confirmed unlocked and working** on my own
physical D3500.

## Unlocked

| # | Capability | Stock D3500 | → | Research build |
|:--:|---|---|:--:|---|
| 1 | **Kelvin white balance** | Presets only (Auto, cloudy, sun…) | → | Manual Kelvin, 2500–10000 K in 31 steps — stills **and** movie |
| 2 | **Active D-Lighting** | On / Off | → | 6 levels (Auto · Extra High · High · Normal · Low · Off) |
| 3 | **Noise reduction** | A single control | → | Long-exposure NR **+** high-ISO NR (4 levels), as separate controls |
| 4 | **ISO granularity** | Coarse steps | → | Fine 1/3-EV steps |
| 5 | **Image quality** | Fewer modes | → | 7 modes, including RAW + JPEG combinations |
| 6 | **JPEG encoding** | Standard quality | → | Fine at the maximum-quality setting (DQT = 1) |
| 7 | **Video** | 29-minute limit | → | No recording-time limit, higher bitrate (up to 48 Mbps), clean LiveView |
| 8 | **On-screen languages** | Few (regional lock) | → | All 31 languages |
| 9 | **Firmware** | C 1.00, stock | → | Custom build (C 6.71) running on real hardware |

## Honest limitations

- These are **EXPEED 4 capabilities already present in sibling bodies** (D5600 / D7200),
  surfaced on the D3500 — **not new hardware**.
- **Physically absent** on the D3500 and impossible to add in software: focus peaking,
  in-body AF motor, built-in Wi-Fi, IR remote receiver, tilt/touch screen, stereo-mic
  jack, ultrasonic sensor cleaning.
- Only **NEF (RAW)** is lossless; **JPEG is always a lossy format**, even at the
  maximum-quality setting.
